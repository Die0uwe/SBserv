SBserv is installed
===================

Start
-----
Open SBserv from the Start menu (or the desktop icon). Press Enter to accept
the default port (8080), then the dashboard opens in its own window.
Menu: [1] open site, [2] files, [3] public link, [4] database logs,
[5] change port, [6] stop link, [7] language, [8] exit, D = dashboard.

Your website
------------
Put your files in the folder "public_html" (Start menu: "SBserv website
folder"). Your own files are never overwritten by updates.

Database and users
------------------
Your data lives in database.db next to sbserv.exe. In the dashboard, open
"Users" to add accounts for your own website. Your pages can use the login API
on the same port as the site:
  POST /api/auth/login   POST /api/auth/logout   GET /api/auth/me
The example page login.html is in your website folder.

Public link (Cloudflare Quick Tunnel)
-------------------------------------
Choose [3] in the menu or "Start tunnel" in the dashboard. The link
changes every time. Quick Tunnels are for testing: no uptime guarantee.
Your PC and SBserv must stay running.

Remove
------
Uninstall via Windows Settings > Apps. You are asked whether to keep your
data (recommended).
